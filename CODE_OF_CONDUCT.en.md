# Code of Conduct · MUSEO 404 · siNOMBRE

🇪🇸 [Versión en español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our commitment

**MUSEO 404 · siNOMBRE** exists to lend spaces to one another, to discover each other, and to help each other exist without competing for attention.

To foster an open, welcoming and safe community, those of us who take part in this repository, in the public museums and in the conversation channels commit to treating with respect everyone who creates, visits, programs, designs or curates within the project, regardless of their experience, identity, gender, sexual orientation, age, origin, disability or socioeconomic status.

---

## Principles of coexistence in the archipelago

1. **Take care of shared spaces (especially Lent Islands)**
   Lending an island is a gesture of mutual trust. Whoever receives a space commits to respecting the agreements with the host; whoever hosts commits to always acknowledging the authorship of the invited work.

2. **Constructive criticism, not disqualification**
   Reviews of code, design or curation focus on the work and on how to improve it, never on attacking the person.

3. **No harassment, doxxing or aggressive commercial exploitation**
   It is forbidden to publish private personal data of third parties, to impersonate authorship (`curatedFrom` or profiles), or to use guestbooks for spam or to intimidate other creators.

4. **Transparency in decisions**
   Decisions that affect the network's rules or the flagship museum (`museo404`) are discussed openly before being applied.

---

## Behaviors we do not tolerate

- Public or private harassment, insults, mockery or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, coordinated attacks against public museums, or vandalism in guestbooks.
- Publishing private information (email addresses, phone numbers, addresses) without explicit permission.
- Illegal content, violent content, or content that incites hatred.

---

## Reporting a problem or conflict

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior in the repository or within MUSEO 404's public spaces:

- You can report it directly through a **private Issue / message to the maintainer team**, or through the official contact channel set up by the community on GitHub.
- Every report will be reviewed seriously, confidentially and with respect for the affected person.
- The maintainers reserve the right to edit or hide harmful content, revoke access, or exclude someone temporarily or permanently if they seriously breach these agreements.
